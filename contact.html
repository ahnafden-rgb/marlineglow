How to order

1. Add products to your cart and tap Checkout.
2. Enter your name, phone and address.
3. Send the delivery charge by bKash or Nagad and enter the Transaction ID.
4. Place your order — we confirm it on WhatsApp.

You can also order directly on WhatsApp, Messenger or by phone.

Delivery information

We are based in Dhaka and deliver all over Bangladesh. Delivery charge: inside Dhaka and outside Dhaka.

Payment

Products are paid in cash on delivery. The delivery charge is paid in advance by bKash or Nagad (Send Money) to 01813575452, and the Transaction ID is entered at checkout.
